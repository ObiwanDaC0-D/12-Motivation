Neural circuits support learning, valuation, action selection, and effort. Neurotransmitters are not simple one-to-one labels for emotions or personality.

BEHAVIOR & HABIT

Design an action loop

A cue can prompt an action, and outcomes can shape future behavior. Habits develop through repeated context-dependent learning; simple repetition does not guarantee automaticity.

Implementation intentions

If–then planning specifies a situational cue and a response. It can improve goal enactment in many settings, but effects depend on context and execution.

SOCIOLOGY OF MOTIVATION

Agency in a social world

People do not pursue goals in a vacuum. Institutions, social roles, resources, norms, and unequal opportunity structures shape what actions are feasible and rewarded.

Structural opportunity model
